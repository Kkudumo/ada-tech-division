Managed IT
Standard

The same seven steps for all of them

Assess, diagnose, recommend, approve, implement, test, document.

  1. Assess

    We ask about the device or system, the symptoms, what changed recently and how much it is stopping your work.

  2. Diagnose

    The parts that could cause that symptom are tested, so the cause is found and not guessed.

  3. Recommend

    You are told what was found and the options: repair, replace, configure, update or refer.

  4. Approve

    Where there is a cost beyond the diagnostic, nothing happens until you have agreed to it.

  5. Implement

    The repair, setup or installation is done to the agreed scope.

  6. Test

    The original symptom is checked again, and the device is tested as a whole.

  7. Document

    Completed work can be handed over with a private Service Record and a route for follow-up.
