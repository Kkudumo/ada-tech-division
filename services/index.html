In short

ADA Tech repairs laptops and desktops, installs Windows and Office, updates and recovers BIOS firmware, sets up Wi-Fi and office networks, gives remote support across Namibia, sets up security and backups, installs CCTV, builds small-business servers and sets up whole offices. Work on a single device starts with a N$99 diagnostic that comes off the repair. Larger jobs are assessed and quoted.
