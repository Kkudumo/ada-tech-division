Why does my Wi-Fi keep disconnecting?

One question splits this problem in half: does every device drop at the same moment, or only one laptop?

Get it diagnosedAsk on WhatsApp
In short

If every device loses the connection together, the fault is in the router, its position or the internet line. If only one laptop drops while phones stay connected, the fault is in that laptop: its Wi-Fi driver, its power-saving setting or its saved network profile. Compare two devices side by side first, then follow the checks for whichever half applies.

Only one laptop drops, phones stay connectedThat laptop's Wi-Fi adapter, driver or power-saving setting.
It works near the router and drops in other roomsSignal strength. Walls, distance and where the router sits.
It drops at the same time each day, often eveningsCongestion, either on your provider's network or on the Wi-Fi channel.
“Connected, no internet”The Wi-Fi itself is fine. The fault is between the router and the internet.
Self-checks

What you can check yourself

These are safe to try, cost nothing and do not need tools.

  1. Compare two devices

    When the laptop drops, look at a phone on the same Wi-Fi. This one check tells you whether to look at the router or the laptop.

  2. Restart the router properly

    Switch the router off at the wall, wait 30 seconds, switch it on and give it three minutes.

  3. Move closer

    Test beside the router. If it is stable there, the problem is coverage, and the router's position matters more than its settings.

  4. Forget the network and rejoin

    Settings, Network & internet, Wi-Fi, Manage known networks. Choose the network, select Forget, then connect again with the password.

  5. Stop Windows switching the adapter off

    Right-click the Start button and open Device Manager. Under Network adapters, open the Wi-Fi adapter, go to Power Management and untick Allow the computer to turn off this device to save power.

  6. Update the Wi-Fi driver

    Download the wireless driver for your exact model from the laptop maker's support page.

  7. Choose the right band

    If the router offers 2.4GHz and 5GHz, 5GHz is faster over a short distance and 2.4GHz reaches further through walls.

  8. Reset the network settings

    Settings, Network & internet, Advanced network settings, Network reset. You will need to enter your Wi-Fi passwords again afterwards.

Get the network assessed if
  • The router is old, runs hot or restarts by itself.
  • An office or lodge needs coverage across several rooms or buildings.
  • The connection has to be reliable for card machines, cameras or bookings.

Questions

Common questions

Is it my internet provider or my router?

If the router's internet light goes out or changes colour when the connection drops, the fault is on the provider's side or the line. If the lights stay normal and devices still drop, look at the router and the Wi-Fi.

Will a Wi-Fi extender fix it?

Sometimes. An extender repeats the signal it receives, so if it is placed where the signal is already weak it repeats a weak signal. A cabled access point is usually more reliable for an office.

Can you fix Wi-Fi remotely?

Laptop settings and drivers, often yes. Router position, cabling and coverage need a visit, which we do in Rundu.
