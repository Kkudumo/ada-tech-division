Connectivity help

Why does my laptop Wi-Fi
keep disconnecting?

The fastest clue is whether every device disconnects or only one laptop does.

If every device disconnects

The router, internet service, signal coverage, power or wider network configuration may be the problem.

If only one laptop disconnects

Check the laptop's Wi-Fi adapter, driver, saved network profile and Windows power settings. A failing adapter can also create intermittent drops.

Signal and interference matter

Distance, walls, crowded wireless channels and poor router placement can produce a connection that works near the router but drops elsewhere.
