In short

These are the faults ADA Tech is asked about most: a laptop that will not turn on, black and blue screens, slow or overheating laptops, Windows Update failures, Wi-Fi that drops, worn batteries, printers that will not print, virus warnings and liquid spills. You do not need to know the cause before asking for help. A standard diagnostic costs N$99 and comes off the repair if you go ahead.

F-01 / Power and startup

My laptop will not turn on

No lights, lights but no picture, or it starts and then stops.

Causes and checksF-02 / Display and startup

The screen is black but the power is on

Power light on, fan running, nothing on the screen.

Causes and checksF-03 / Windows

It keeps showing a blue screen

Windows stops with an error and a stop code, then restarts.

Causes and checksF-04 / Performance

My laptop is very slow

Slow to start, slow to open programs, freezes when busy.

Causes and checksF-05 / Cooling

It overheats or the fan is always loud

Hot to touch, loud fan, slows down or switches itself off.

Causes and checksF-06 / Windows

Windows Update fails or gets stuck

Stuck on a percentage, fails with a code, or keeps undoing changes.

Causes and checksF-07 / Network

The Wi-Fi keeps disconnecting

Drops out, reconnects, or says connected with no internet.

Causes and checksF-08 / Power

The battery drains fast or will not charge

An hour of use, “plugged in, not charging”, or it dies at 30%.

Causes and checksF-09 / Printers

The printer will not print

Shows offline, jobs stuck in the queue, or prints nothing.

Causes and checksF-10 / Security

Pop-ups, virus warnings or strange behaviour

Warnings that will not close, new toolbars, programs you did not install.

Causes and checksF-11 / Emergency

I spilled liquid on my laptop

Water, tea, a soft drink or beer went into the keyboard.

Causes and checksF-12 / Upgrades

Should I upgrade the RAM or the SSD?

Which upgrade makes an older laptop faster, and which is wasted money.
