Why is my laptop so slow?

Slow is a symptom with four common causes. Task Manager shows which one you have in about two minutes, and that decides whether the fix is free, cheap or a part.

Get it diagnosedAsk on WhatsApp
In short

Most slow laptops have one of four causes: a mechanical hard drive instead of an SSD, too little memory (RAM) for what is open, too many programs starting with Windows, or a drive that is nearly full. Open Task Manager with Ctrl + Shift + Esc and look at the Performance tab. A disk at 100% points to the drive, and memory above about 85% points to RAM.

What you see, and what it usually means

What you seeWhat it usually means
Disk shows 100% in Task Manager, mostly after starting upA mechanical hard drive (HDD), or a drive that is failing.
Memory is above about 85% with only a few programs openNot enough RAM for how you use it.
Slow for the first few minutes, then fineToo many programs starting with Windows.
It slows down when it gets hot and the fan is loudHeat. The processor slows itself to cool down. See overheating.
Less than about 15% of drive C: is freeThe drive is too full for Windows to work properly.
It became slow suddenly, with pop-ups or unknown programsUnwanted software. See virus and pop-ups.
Self-checks

What you can check yourself

These are safe to try, cost nothing and do not need tools.

  1. Restart, do not just shut down

    Choose Restart from the power menu. On Windows 10 and 11, Shut down saves part of the system to start faster next time, so only Restart gives a clean start.

  2. Read Task Manager

    Press Ctrl + Shift + Esc and open the Performance tab. Look at Memory and Disk. Under Disk, Windows shows whether the drive is an SSD or an HDD.

  3. Turn off startup programs

    In Task Manager, open Startup apps. Disable anything you do not need the moment the laptop starts. Nothing is uninstalled by doing this.

  4. Free up space

    Open Settings, System, Storage and follow the clean-up recommendations. Aim to keep at least 15% of the drive free.

  5. Remove what you do not use

    Uninstall programs you no longer use, and keep only one antivirus. Windows Security is already built in.

  6. Scan for unwanted software

    Open Windows Security, Virus & threat protection, Scan options and run a Full scan.

  7. Install pending updates

    A laptop that is part-way through updates can be slow until they finish.

Stop and get it looked at if
  • The drive clicks or grinds.
  • Files will not open, or go missing.
  • It freezes completely for a minute or more at a time.

These point to a failing drive. Copy your important files off it before trying anything else.

Questions

Common questions

Will an SSD or more RAM make it faster?

Usually one of them will, but which one depends on the bottleneck. See RAM or SSD: which upgrade first?

Will reinstalling Windows make it faster?

It helps when the cause is years of software clutter. It does not change a mechanical hard drive or too little memory, so check those first.

Do I need a new laptop?

Often not. A laptop that has a mechanical hard drive and 4GB of memory usually improves a great deal with an SSD and more RAM, if the processor is reasonably recent. See repair or replace.
