Fault F-08Power

Why is my laptop battery draining so fast?

Windows can tell you exactly how worn the battery is. One command produces a report that separates a worn battery from a setting or a charger fault.

Get it diagnosedAsk on WhatsApp
In short

Laptop batteries wear out with use. To see how worn yours is, open Command Prompt and run powercfg /batteryreport, then compare “Full charge capacity” with “Design capacity” in the report. If the battery now holds much less than it was designed to, it needs replacing. If the capacity is healthy, look at screen brightness, background programs and the charger.

What you see, and what it usually means

What you seeWhat it usually means
It used to last four hours and now lasts oneThe battery has worn. Check the capacity in the battery report.
It switches off suddenly at 20% or 30%Worn cells. The percentage shown is no longer accurate.
“Plugged in, not charging”The charger's wattage, the charging port, the battery, or a charge limit set by the maker's own app.
It stops charging at 60% or 80% every timeUsually a battery-care setting from the manufacturer, which is deliberate and protects the battery.
The battery drains while the lid is closedThe laptop is waking up in sleep. Use Hibernate or Shut down instead.
The case bulges, or the touchpad is liftingA swollen battery. Stop using and charging it.
Self-checks

What you can check yourself

These are safe to try, cost nothing and do not need tools.

  1. Run the battery report

    Press the Windows key, type cmd and press Enter. Run:

    powercfg /batteryreport

    Windows saves a file called battery-report.html and shows where. Open it and compare Design capacity with Full charge capacity.

  2. See what is using the battery

    Settings, System, Power & battery, Battery usage lists the programs that used the most power.

  3. Lower the screen brightness

    The screen is usually the largest drain. Lower it, and choose Best power efficiency as the power mode.

  4. Check the charger

    Use the original charger or one with the same wattage. A lower-wattage charger can run the laptop without charging the battery.

  5. Look for a charge limit

    Lenovo, ASUS, Dell, HP and others include a battery-care option in their own app that stops charging at 60% or 80% on purpose.

Stop and get it looked at if
  • The battery is swollen. Do not charge it, press on it or pierce it.
  • The charger or the charging port gets very hot or sparks.
  • You smell burning near the charging port.

Questions

Common questions

How long should a laptop battery last?

A battery wears a little with every charge. After a few years of daily use, holding noticeably less than when new is normal. The battery report shows the real figure for yours.

Is it bad to leave the laptop plugged in all the time?

Modern laptops stop charging when full, so it is safe. If you mostly work plugged in, the maker's battery-care setting, which holds the charge at about 80%, slows the wear.

Can I keep using a swollen battery?

No. A swollen battery can catch fire. Switch the laptop off, do not charge it, and have the battery removed.
