Format

How to read a case file

  1. Issue

    What the client reported, in their words.

  2. Diagnosis

    What testing showed the cause to be.

  3. Intervention

    The work that was agreed and done.

  4. Result

    What the device or system does now.

Tell us what is going wrong.
