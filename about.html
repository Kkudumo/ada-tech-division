The bench sequence

Seven steps from fault to handover

The aim is work that can be explained: less guessing, and no surprises on the bill.

  1. Assess

    We ask about the device or system, the symptoms, what changed recently and how much it is stopping your work.

  2. Diagnose

    The parts that could cause that symptom are tested, so the cause is found and not guessed.

  3. Recommend

    You are told what was found and the options: repair, replace, configure, update or refer.

  4. Approve

    Where there is a cost beyond the diagnostic, nothing happens until you have agreed to it.

  5. Implement

    The repair, setup or installation is done to the agreed scope.

  6. Test

    The original symptom is checked again, and the device is tested as a whole.

  7. Document

    Completed work can be handed over with a private Service Record and a route for follow-up.

Handover

What a Service Record is

After a completed job, ADA Tech can issue a private digital record of the device, the service performed, a summary of the work, the result and any follow-up note.

  • It opens with a record code and a private token, and is not searchable.
  • From it you can leave a review that is verified against the job.
  • From it you can ask for follow-up with the original job reference attached.
  • It can be printed or saved as a PDF.
