Tell us what is going wrong

You do not need to know whether the cause is hardware, Windows, the BIOS or the Wi-Fi. Give us the symptom and we start from there.

Four useful details

What helps us diagnose faster

  1. Device or system

    Laptop make and model, desktop, router, printer, office network or other system.

  2. Symptom

    What exactly happens: an error message, a blue screen, no power, slow, dropping connection.

  3. When it started

    After an update, a fall, a power cut, new software or a hardware change, or for no clear reason.

  4. Impact

    Whether the device is still usable, or work has stopped.
