What helps us diagnose faster

Four useful details.

1. Device or system
Laptop model, desktop, router, printer, office network or other system.
2. Symptom
What exactly is happening? Error message, blue screen, no power, slow speed, disconnection, etc.
3. When it started
After an update, fall, power issue, new software, hardware change—or no obvious reason.
4. Impact
Is the device still usable, or has work stopped completely?
