ADA First Aid: monthly care for your computer

For people whose laptop or desktop keeps needing small fixes. A known person to call, a monthly check, and a discount when something bigger goes wrong.

See the three plansI need a one-time repair
In short

ADA First Aid is a monthly care plan for personal laptops and desktops. It covers remote support, monthly health checks and help with Windows, updates, drivers and everyday software, with 10% or 15% off other ADA Tech labour. Plans cost N$249, N$399 and N$699 a month. It is not insurance: parts, paid licences and major physical repairs are separate.

Questions

Questions about First Aid

Is First Aid insurance?

No. It is a support relationship. It does not include unlimited physical repairs, replacement parts or paid software licences.

Can I use it outside Rundu?

Yes. The support in the plans is remote, so it works anywhere in Namibia with an internet connection. Physical repairs still need the workshop in Rundu.

How many remote sessions are included?

Essential includes up to three a month and Family includes six. Plus covers reasonable use during business hours. If you are not sure whether something is covered, ask before the session starts.

My laptop is broken now. Can I subscribe to get it fixed?

Start with a one-time diagnosis and repair. First Aid is for keeping a working device working. Once it is repaired, a plan makes sense.
